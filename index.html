Private desktop recruiting assistant

Operation Get Lucas a Job

A local-first tool for organizing an engineering job search: reviewing public job opportunities, ranking fit, tracking application evidence, and preparing recruiting briefings.

Single-user project

This project is designed for its operator's personal job search, not for public signups or a commercial recruiting service. The public pages here explain its purpose and privacy practices; the actual application runs on the operator's Windows computer.

What the application does

  • Reads publicly available job listings and builds local, reviewable job shortlists.
  • Organizes application status evidence and prioritizes follow-up tasks.
  • Offers an optional Gmail integration to examine recruiting-related email and identify application confirmations, interview invitations, and other job-search updates.
  • Provides local recruiting reports and manual review controls.

Why it requests Gmail access

When separately authorized, the Gmail integration requests read-only Gmail access so the operator can identify recruiting correspondence and reconcile application history. It does not require permission to send, modify, or delete messages. The application never sends email automatically through this read-only integration.

Google access is optional and can be revoked from the operator's Google Account. The local project's master STOP and permission checks are separate safeguards.

Privacy and security

Read the Privacy Policy for the types of data handled, local storage practices, and how to revoke access or delete locally stored data.
