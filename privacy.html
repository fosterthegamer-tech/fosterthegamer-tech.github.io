Privacy Policy

Operation Get Lucas a Job · Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy describes the Gmail-connected component of Operation Get Lucas a Job, a private, local-first job-search tool intended for use by its operator. The public informational website does not require visitors to connect their Google accounts or enter personal information.

Google information accessed

If the operator explicitly authorizes the Gmail integration, the application requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. That permission allows read-only access to Gmail messages and related metadata. The application uses it to identify job-application confirmations, recruiter communications, interview scheduling, and other relevant hiring correspondence.

How Google data is used

Recruiting-related message contents may be inspected transiently to extract structured facts such as employer, job title, requisition reference, application stage, and correspondence date. Structured results can be held in local preview files and, when explicitly approved, incorporated into local application-history records. The Gmail import is designed to minimize retention of complete email bodies.

Storage and retention

The application runs locally on the operator's computer. Google OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored in private local configuration files, not on this public website. Application-history evidence and derived recruiting details may be retained locally until the operator removes the relevant files or records. Local backups, if any, must be managed by the operator.

Sharing and transfers

The current read-only Gmail import is designed to process email locally. This public website does not receive Gmail messages, OAuth tokens, or application-history records. The application does not sell Google user data or use Gmail data for advertising. Any future feature that sends Gmail-derived content to a separate AI provider or remote service must be separately authorized, accurately disclosed, and reflected in an updated privacy notice before activation.

Permissions and safeguards

Gmail access is optional. The read-only integration cannot send, modify, or delete Gmail messages using its granted scope. Recruiting actions such as applying to jobs, sending messages, or making LinkedIn connections are outside this Gmail read-only permission and require separate controls and authorization.

Revocation and deletion

The operator may revoke Google account access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Revocation stops new authorized access but does not automatically delete locally saved application-history evidence; local records and tokens must be deleted separately through the project's maintenance procedures.

Contact and changes

For questions about this personal-use application and its privacy practices, use the user-support email shown on its Google OAuth consent screen. This page will be updated before any material change to the application's handling of Google user data.
